Outdoor obstacle. Zig zag. Move plank to plank as they rise, keeping your rhythm and balance, then come off the end under control. Here is the technique step by step. Before you start, look at the full route and decide which foot goes first. Then, Put the lead foot in the centre of the next plank and drive up through it. Then, Keep your arms out for balance and your eyes on the next plank, not your feet. Then, Stay low and centred. The planks are narrow, so shorten your step. Then, Lower yourself onto the next plank with a bent support knee. Then, From the lowest safe exit, jump forward and land on both feet with bent knees.
