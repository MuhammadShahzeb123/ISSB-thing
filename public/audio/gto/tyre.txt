Outdoor obstacle. Tyre.

Pass through a tyre hanging at about waist-to-chest height, feet first, and land on your feet on the other side.

Here is the technique, step by step.

Step 1. Approach square on. Walk up to the tyre with it directly in front of you.

Step 2. Grip the tyre. Hold the inner rim at the sides or top. Keep your arms bent and your body close.

Step 3. Spring and lift the legs. Bend your knees, spring and lift both legs together in front of you.

Step 4. Feet first through. Push both feet straight through the hole with your toes pointed.

Step 5. Lie back and slide the hips through. Lean back with your weight on your hands and slide your hips through the tyre.

Step 6. Head and shoulders follow. Tuck your chin and let your shoulders follow, holding the rim until your feet reach down.

Step 7. Land on your feet. Let go, land on both feet with bent knees and move on.
