Outdoor obstacle. Boxing ring. Three ropes in a row, jump over the first, go under the second and jump over the third without touching them. Here is the technique step by step. Build an even rhythm. Speed is not needed here. Then, Lead with a straight leg and lift the trail knee up and sideways. Then, Bend at the knees and hips before you reach the middle rope. Keep your head down. Then, Take short steps with your back flat. Stay low until your hips are clear. Then, Stand up only once you are past the rope, then take two quick strides. Then, Use the same technique as rope one. Land running and move straight on.
