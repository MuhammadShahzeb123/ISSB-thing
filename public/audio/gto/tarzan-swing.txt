Outdoor obstacle. Tarzan swing. Take the rope on a raised platform, swing out with momentum and let go at the forward end so you land inside the marked target. Here is the technique step by step. Grip at head height with your hands close together, strong hand on top. Then, Bend your knees with your weight on the balls of your feet and look at the target. Then, Push forward off the platform and hold on firmly. Then, Pull your knees up so your feet clear the ground at the lowest point. Then, As you rise, shoot your legs forward toward the target. Then, Let go with both hands together just before the rope stops rising. Then, Land on both feet inside the marked area with your knees bent.
