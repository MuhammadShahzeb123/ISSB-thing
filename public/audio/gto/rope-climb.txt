Outdoor obstacle. Rope climb. Climb a vertical rope using a leg lock so your legs do most of the work, touch the top and come down under control. Here is the technique step by step. Stand with the rope in front of you, almost touching your chest. Then, Reach high and grip with one hand above the other, thumbs wrapped around the rope. Then, Pull your knees up. Let the rope run down the outside of one leg and across the top of that foot, then clamp it with the other foot J hook or S wrap. Then, Straighten your legs to push your body up. The hands only hold, while the legs lift. Then, Move one hand higher, then the other. Keep your elbows close to your body. Then, Knees up, lock, stand, reach. Keep a steady rhythm and do not rush. Then, Touch the beam or the mark clearly so the officer can see it. Then, Loosen the foot lock a little so it acts as a brake. Lower yourself hand under hand and never slide.
