Outdoor obstacle. Rope climb.

Climb a vertical rope using a leg lock so your legs do most of the work, touch the top and come down under control.

Here is the technique, step by step.

Step 1. Stand close to the rope. Stand with the rope in front of you, almost touching your chest.

Step 2. High grip. Reach high and grip with one hand above the other, thumbs wrapped around the rope.

Step 3. Knees up and lock the feet. Pull your knees up. Let the rope run down the outside of one leg and across the top of that foot, then clamp it with the other foot (J-hook or S-wrap).

Step 4. Stand up on the lock. Straighten your legs to push your body up. The hands only hold, while the legs lift.

Step 5. Hand over hand. Move one hand higher, then the other. Keep your elbows close to your body.

Step 6. Repeat the inchworm. Knees up, lock, stand, reach. Keep a steady rhythm and do not rush.

Step 7. Touch the top. Touch the beam or the mark clearly so the officer can see it.

Step 8. Controlled descent. Loosen the foot lock a little so it acts as a brake. Lower yourself hand under hand and never slide.
