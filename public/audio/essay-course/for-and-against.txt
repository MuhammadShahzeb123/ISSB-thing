Lesson eleven. The for and against essay.

Let us pull the structure together. The book sums it up in one line. Your essay needs a beginning, a middle and an end. In that order.

Many essay titles ask you to discuss a statement, weigh up the arguments, or make your own point of view clear. The book calls these discursive essays. You put forward the arguments for and against a point of view, and then you reach a judgement.

The examiner is checking a few things. Are you clear and concise? Are your points well organised? Do you balance the arguments for and against? And does the essay have a sound overall structure?

The book warns that these essays can easily ramble. So keep a tight hold on your argument. Back up each point with detailed examples. And do not ignore the other side. Bring in the counter arguments and deal with them.

The book's sample essay discusses whether watching professional sport has become far too important for many people. Look at how its paragraphs open. However, it is not only the television viewers who are the fanatics. On the other hand, such an obsession may come from a lack of close relationships. Then a paragraph on the role of the media, linked by the words this obsession. Then, it can be argued, then. Then Nevertheless. And finally, In conclusion, then, I would argue.

See how the essay moves from one side to the other, yet every paragraph is linked to the last. And the conclusion leaves no doubt about the writer's own view.

The sample also uses a couple of rhetorical questions. These are questions you ask so that you can go on to answer them. The book says they are useful, but do not overdo them.

So a weak discursive essay rambles from point to point and never says what the writer thinks. A better one weighs each side with examples, deals with the strongest counter argument, and ends with a clear judgement.

Your turn. Take the book's title about banning smoking in all public places. List two arguments for and two against, the counter argument you would answer, and your own final view in one sentence.
