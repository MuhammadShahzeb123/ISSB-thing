Captain Muhammad Sarwar. Captain Muhammad Sarwar led an attack on a fortified position at Tilpatra in Kashmir. Barbed wire stopped his company close to the defenders. Radio Pakistan records that, despite a shoulder wound, he moved forward with six men and cut the wire under fire. The opening allowed the attack to continue towards its objective. He was killed by machine gun fire on 27 July 1948. Remember Sarwar, wounded shoulder, wire cut, way forward.
