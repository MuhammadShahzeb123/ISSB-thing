Sawar Muhammad Hussain. Sawar Muhammad Hussain served as a driver with 20th Lancers. Near Harar Khurd, he spotted enemy tanks by a minefield and directed recoilless rifle fire onto them. ISPR credits the resulting fire with destroying tanks and strengthening the local defence. He was killed by machine gun fire while directing the guns on 10 December 1971. Remember Hussain, the driver who spotted tanks and guided fire.
