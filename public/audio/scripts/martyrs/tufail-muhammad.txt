Major Tufail Muhammad. Major Tufail Muhammad led an assault on an Indian post at Lakshmipur in then East Pakistan. Radio Pakistan describes how he divided his men into three groups and attacked in darkness. Despite bullet wounds in his abdomen, he used grenades against machine gun positions and kept directing the attack. His men drove the opposing troops out of the post. He died from his wounds on 7 August 1958. Remember Tufail, wounded commander, grenades, post cleared.
