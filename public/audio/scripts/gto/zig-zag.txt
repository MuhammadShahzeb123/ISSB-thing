Outdoor obstacle. Zig-zag.

Move plank to plank as they rise, keeping your rhythm and balance, then come off the end under control.

Here is the technique, step by step.

Step 1. Read the planks. Before you start, look at the full route and decide which foot goes first.

Step 2. Step up and keep moving. Put the lead foot in the centre of the next plank and drive up through it.

Step 3. Arms out, eyes forward. Keep your arms out for balance and your eyes on the next plank, not your feet.

Step 4. The highest plank. Stay low and centred. The planks are narrow, so shorten your step.

Step 5. Step down. Lower yourself onto the next plank with a bent support knee.

Step 6. Jump off and land. From the lowest safe exit, jump forward and land on both feet with bent knees.
