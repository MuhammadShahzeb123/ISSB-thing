Outdoor obstacle. Plank walk. Climb up, walk a raised narrow plank, step up onto the victory stand, stand upright for a moment, then walk off and climb down. Here is the technique step by step. Keep three points of contact, two hands and one foot, or two feet and one hand. Then, Fix your eyes on the far end and hold your arms out for balance. Then, Use short, even steps and place your feet in line along the plank. Then, Step up with one foot, drive through it and bring the other foot up. Then, Stand fully upright for a moment so the officer can see it, then continue. Then, Step down under control and keep the same steady pace. Then, Go down one rung at a time. Do not jump from the top.
