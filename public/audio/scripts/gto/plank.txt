Outdoor obstacle. Plank walk.

Climb up, walk a raised narrow plank, step up onto the victory stand, stand upright for a moment, then walk off and climb down.

Here is the technique, step by step.

Step 1. Climb the ladder. Keep three points of contact: two hands and one foot, or two feet and one hand.

Step 2. Stand tall on the plank. Fix your eyes on the far end and hold your arms out for balance.

Step 3. Walk the plank. Use short, even steps and place your feet in line along the plank.

Step 4. Mount the victory stand. Step up with one foot, drive through it and bring the other foot up.

Step 5. Stand upright. Stand fully upright for a moment so the officer can see it, then continue.

Step 6. Step down and walk on. Step down under control and keep the same steady pace.

Step 7. Climb down. Go down one rung at a time. Do not jump from the top.
