Outdoor obstacle. Tyre. Pass through a tyre hanging at about waist to chest height, feet first, and land on your feet on the other side. Here is the technique step by step. Walk up to the tyre with it directly in front of you. Then, Hold the inner rim at the sides or top. Keep your arms bent and your body close. Then, Bend your knees, spring and lift both legs together in front of you. Then, Push both feet straight through the hole with your toes pointed. Then, Lean back with your weight on your hands and slide your hips through the tyre. Then, Tuck your chin and let your shoulders follow, holding the rim until your feet reach down. Then, Let go, land on both feet with bent knees and move on.
