Outdoor obstacle. Long jump, the ditch.

Clear a wide ditch from a short run-up. Speed and a committed take-off from the near edge matter more than height.

Here is the technique, step by step.

Step 1. Mark and get ready. Know your take-off foot. Start from a spot that lets that foot land on the edge naturally.

Step 2. Accelerate. Run in a controlled rhythm and keep your eyes on the far bank, not the ditch.

Step 3. Plant on the edge. Keep your speed. Put the whole take-off foot down flat, just behind the edge.

Step 4. Drive up and forward. Push hard through the take-off leg and drive the free knee and both arms up. Aim for a low, forward flight of roughly 20 to 25 degrees, not a high hop.

Step 5. Hold the flight. Keep your chest up and bring both legs forward together.

Step 6. Reach and land. Reach your heels forward, land on both feet and swing your arms forward.

Step 7. Absorb forward. Bend your knees and let your weight roll forward, never backward. Then move on to the next obstacle.
