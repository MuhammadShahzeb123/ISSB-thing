Outdoor obstacle. Long jump ditch. Clear a wide ditch from a short run up. Speed and a committed take off from the near edge matter more than height. Here is the technique step by step. Know your take off foot. Start from a spot that lets that foot land on the edge naturally. Then, Run in a controlled rhythm and keep your eyes on the far bank, not the ditch. Then, Keep your speed. Put the whole take off foot down flat, just behind the edge. Then, Push hard through the take off leg and drive the free knee and both arms up. Aim for a low, forward flight of roughly 20 25°, not a high hop. Then, Keep your chest up and bring both legs forward together. Then, Reach your heels forward, land on both feet and swing your arms forward. Then, Bend your knees and let your weight roll forward, never backward. Then move on to the next obstacle.
