Outdoor obstacle. Double rope.

Cross from one platform to the other on two parallel ropes, with your feet on the lower rope and your hands on the upper rope.

Here is the technique, step by step.

Step 1. Grip the upper rope. Take the upper rope in both hands before stepping on. Keep your hands about shoulder-width apart.

Step 2. Step onto the lower rope. Put the arch of the foot on the rope, not the toes, with your knees slightly bent.

Step 3. Lead foot slides. Slide the lead foot along the rope. Never cross your feet.

Step 4. Hand follows. Slide the lead hand after the foot so three points always stay in contact.

Step 5. Close up and repeat. Bring the trail foot and hand up, then repeat: lead foot, lead hand, close. Keep your hips close to the rope and look ahead.

Step 6. Step off onto the platform. Release only once both feet are on the platform.
