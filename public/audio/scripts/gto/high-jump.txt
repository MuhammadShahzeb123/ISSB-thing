Outdoor obstacle. High jump. Clear a bar set across two poles using the scissors technique. You take off from one foot and land on your feet, not your back. Here is the technique step by step. Use 5 to 7 springy strides so you arrive balanced, not flat out. Then, Take off from the foot farther from the bar. Lean back very slightly as you plant. Then, Kick the lead leg straight up and over the bar while both arms drive up. Then, The lead leg comes down as the trail leg lifts over. Keep your hips high and your chest upright. Then, Land on the lead foot, then both feet, with knees bent. Keep your arms out for balance.
