Gaza after a year of ceasefire.

Before dawn on 16 September 2026, a building with 6 floors began to crack in Gaza City. Many people were helped out. Then it fell.

Gaza's Civil Defence said 21 people died. Health officials said 12 of them were children. The building had been damaged in the war, but families had nowhere better to live.

This happened almost 1 year into a ceasefire, an agreement to stop fighting. It began on 10 October 2025, under a 20-point plan from US President Donald Trump.

The war began with the Hamas-led attack on Israel on 7 October 2023. About 1,200 people were killed in Israel and 251 were taken hostage, or held by force, the BBC reported.

By 20 September 2026, Gaza's Health Ministry had counted 73,914 Palestinians killed in Israel's war on Gaza, the UN agency UNRWA reported.

The first part of the deal mostly worked. On 13 October 2025, Hamas freed the last 20 living hostages. Israel freed 1,968 Palestinian prisoners and detainees.

That day, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif joined more than 20 world leaders at a summit in Sharm el-Sheikh, Egypt.

On 17 November 2025, the UN Security Council backed the plan with 13 votes, including Pakistan's. It welcomed a Board of Peace, chaired by Trump, to oversee Gaza for a time.

It also allowed an International Stabilization Force, a group of foreign troops to keep order. On 19 February 2026, Indonesia, Morocco, Kazakhstan, Kosovo and Albania pledged troops.

Pakistan joined the Board of Peace on 22 January 2026. It was not among the countries that pledged troops. Pakistan can join a peacekeeping mission, the Foreign Office said, but it will not join a mission to disarm Hamas.

On 30 July 2026, Hamas agreed, with conditions, to a 15-point roadmap, or step-by-step plan. Its weapons would be handed over in stages while Israeli troops pull back.

On 9 August 2026, Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu rejected the plan. He said his troops will not withdraw until Hamas is fully disarmed.

In August 2026, Israeli forces controlled more than 60 percent of Gaza. More than 250,000 Gazans have applied to join a new police force. A 2.45 billion dollar rebuilding plan has no start date yet.

The killing has not fully stopped. Gaza's Health Ministry counted 1,394 Palestinians killed between the ceasefire and 20 September 2026. Israel says it acts against threats to its forces.

In the occupied West Bank, the UN chief called record settler attacks and settlement growth unlawful. Settlers are Israelis living on occupied Palestinian land.

For Pakistanis, Gaza is not a distant story. Pakistan wants an independent, viable Palestinian state on the pre-1967 borders, with Al-Quds Al-Sharif as its capital. Pakistan does not recognise Israel.

A mature view holds 2 truths together. Every civilian life matters. And a ceasefire without justice is only a pause.

Aid must flow freely, the roadmap must move, and the law must apply to all. Less bombing is good, but a child in Gaza also needs a roof, a school and a future.

Points to remember.

The Gaza ceasefire began on 10 October 2025 under Trump's 20-point plan.
On 13 October 2025 the last 20 living hostages were freed and Israel released 1,968 Palestinians.
UN Security Council Resolution 2803 passed on 17 November 2025 with 13 votes. China and Russia abstained.
