Pakistan leads the SCO as Russia ties move slowly.

On 1 September 2026, in Bishkek, the capital of Kyrgyzstan, Pakistan took over the rotating chair of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation, or SCO, for 1 year. Islamabad will host the group's next summit in 2027. The SCO also named Lahore its tourism and cultural capital for 2026 to 2027.

The SCO is a regional group set up in 2001 by China, Russia and 4 Central Asian states. Pakistan and India joined in 2017, Iran in 2023 and Belarus in 2024. It now has 10 members.

Shehbaz Sharif chose the theme "Turning Vision into Action", with a focus on connectivity, innovation and shared prosperity. He said more than 3 billion people live in the SCO region. For him, better road, rail and energy links are a necessity, not an option.

A year earlier, the summit was in Tianjin, China. There, on 1 September 2025, all members signed a declaration, or joint statement. It condemned the Pahalgam attack. It also condemned the Jaffar Express train attack of 11 March 2025 and the Khuzdar school bus bombing of 21 May 2025 in Pakistan. India and Pakistan both signed it.

Russia is a key SCO member. On 2 September 2025, Shehbaz Sharif met President Vladimir Putin in Beijing. Putin called Pakistan "our traditional partner in Asia". Shehbaz said Pakistan respects Russia's ties with India but wants strong ties of its own.

Trade, however, is small. Pakistan's energy minister said it was about 243 million dollars in 2024 to 2025. It had crossed 1 billion dollars a year earlier. Payment and banking problems pulled it down. Both sides want to revive Pakistan Steel Mills in Karachi, a symbol of their old partnership.

Shehbaz Sharif planned to visit Moscow in early March 2026, but the war in Iran forced a delay. Shehbaz and Putin met again in Bishkek on 31 August 2026.

At the Bishkek summit, Shehbaz said water "must never be weaponised", or used as a weapon. It was a message linked to the Indus Waters dispute with India. On 30 September 2026, Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar promised an "open, inclusive and constructive" chairmanship. Indian officials joined the first meeting of senior SCO officials in Islamabad that week.

Why does this matter? For 1 year, Pakistan will lead the work of a group that includes China, Russia, India and Iran. Better rail and energy routes through Central Asia could open new markets for Pakistani goods.

The sensible view is practical. The SCO's own secretary general says the group is not part of any camp. Pakistan should treat it that way. It can chair the SCO and still work closely with the United States. With Russia, small real steps in energy, rail and steel matter more than big speeches.

Hosting the SCO is an honour. Getting real results for ordinary people is the real test.

Points to remember.

Pakistan took the SCO chair for 2026 to 2027 in Bishkek on 1 September 2026.
Islamabad will host the SCO summit in 2027. Lahore is the SCO tourism and cultural capital for 2026 to 2027.
Pakistan's theme is "Turning Vision into Action".
