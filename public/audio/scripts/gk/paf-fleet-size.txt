How many planes does the PAF have? There is no single official public “PAF has exactly N aircraft” number. Open source directories IISS Military Balance, FlightGlobal World Air Forces, Wikipedia aggregations give bands. For ISSB, state the source, the year, and whether you mean combat aircraft only or all fixed wing types. Combat or fighter band commonly cited from IISS Military Balance 2025 style tallies, on the order of ~400 fighters roughly JF 17 ~150 175, F 16 ~75, J 10CE ~20+, Mirage III or 5 ~80 combined, F 7PG ~50 figures move with deliveries and retirements. Next, Wider “all aircraft” counts on secondary sites sometimes exceed 1,000 when trainers, helicopters including other services, and UAVs are mixed in do not blend those without saying so. Next, Transport snapshot, C 130 ~20+, Il 78 = 4, CN 235 handful again estimates. Next, AEW&C, Saab 2000 Erieye often listed around ~9 in open sources. Why interviewers ask this. Honesty about uncertainty scores better than a fake exact number.
