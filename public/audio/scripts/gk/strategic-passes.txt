Strategic mountain passes Pakistan’s mountain frontiers are pierced by a few named passes that keep appearing in ISSB papers and interviews. Learn where each one sits and which neighbour it serves. Khyber Pass KP or Afghanistan Torkham. Historic military & trade corridor to Kabul. Next, Bolan Pass Balochistan, near Quetta. Historic route toward southern Afghanistan, railway or road significance. Next, Khojak Pass near Chaman, Balochistan Afghanistan frontier. Next, Khunjerab Pass Gilgit Baltistan or Xinjiang China. Highest paved international border crossing on the Karakoram Highway. Next, Lowari Pass or Tunnel Chitral access within KP internal connectivity, not a foreign border. Remember Afghanistan, Khyber, Khojak, Bolan axis. China, Khunjerab. Always name the province or region. Why interviewers ask this. Short lists score well.
