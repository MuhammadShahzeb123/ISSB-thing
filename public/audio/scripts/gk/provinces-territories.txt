Provinces & territories Pakistan’s provincial structure is a staple ISSB question. Know the four provinces and their capitals, plus Islamabad Capital Territory. When asked about the north, distinguish carefully between provinces and administered territories. Punjab capital Lahore. Most populous province, Indus plain agriculture. Next, Sindh capital Karachi also Pakistan’s main port city. Next, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa formerly NWFP or “Sarhad” capital Peshawar. Includes former FATA agencies after the 25th Amendment merger 2018. Next, Balochistan capital Quetta. Largest by area, sparse population, Gwadar on the Makran coast. Next, Islamabad Capital Territory federal capital. Next, Former FATA agencies for recall, Bajaur, Mohmand, Khyber, Kurram, Orakzai, North Waziristan, South Waziristan. Remember Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta + Islamabad. KP = old NWFP, FATA merged into KP 2018. Why interviewers ask this. Fast recall of province capitals is a common warm up question before deeper geopolitics.
