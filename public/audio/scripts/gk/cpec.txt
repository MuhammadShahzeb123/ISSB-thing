CPEC China Pakistan Economic Corridor CPEC is the China Pakistan Economic Corridor, a flagship of China’s Belt and Road Initiative. Phase I focused on energy plants, highways, and Gwadar linked infrastructure. Phase II “CPEC 2.0” emphasises industrialisation, Special Economic Zones SEZs, agriculture, mining, ICT, and business to business investment rather than only mega construction. Core idea, connect China’s Xinjiang via Karakoram Highway or Khunjerab through Pakistan to the Arabian Sea at Gwadar. Next, Early harvest, power generation capacity additions, motorways or highways, Gwadar port and airport related works.
