Borders & neighbours Pakistan has four land neighbours and a southern maritime frontier on the Arabian Sea. Border answers should name the neighbour, the general direction, and one strategic note without inventing lengths or troop figures. India east or south east, includes the Line of Control LoC in Jammu & Kashmir a ceasefire line, not an agreed international boundary. Next, Afghanistan west or north west, Durand Line frontier, key crossings include Torkham via Khyber and Chaman. Next, Iran west or south west, Balochistan frontier, trade and security links via Taftan and coastal routes. Next, China north east, high Karakoram frontier, Khunjerab Pass links to Xinjiang Karakoram Highway. Next, Arabian Sea south, Karachi and Port Qasim Sindh, Gwadar and the Makran coast Balochistan. Remember India Afghanistan Iran China + Arabian Sea. Torkham or Chaman to Afghanistan, Khunjerab to China. Why interviewers ask this.
