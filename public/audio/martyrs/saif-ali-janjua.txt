Naik Saif Ali Janjua. Naik Saif Ali Janjua commanded a platoon defending a post at Bhudha Khanna in Kashmir. The Army biography records that he led his men through repeated attacks and helped repel attempts to take the post. This kept the local position defended while the fighting continued. He was seriously wounded in the battle and died on 26 October 1948. Remember Janjua, platoon leader holding a Kashmir post.
