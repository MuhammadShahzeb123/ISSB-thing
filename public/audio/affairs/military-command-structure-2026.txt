How Pakistan rebuilt its top military command.

On 27 July 2026, a guard of honour waited at General Headquarters, or GHQ, in Rawalpindi. General Syed Aamer Raza laid a wreath at the martyrs' monument. Then he went inside to meet Field Marshal Asim Munir.

That quiet ceremony filled the last big gap in a new military command system. The story starts in May 2025.

After the conflict with India that month, the federal cabinet met on 20 May 2025. It promoted army chief General Asim Munir to Field Marshal, the army's highest rank. Reuters said it was the first such promotion in almost 60 years.

Next came a change to the Constitution. On 13 November 2025, President Asif Ali Zardari signed the 27th Amendment. It rewrote Article 243, which deals with command of the armed forces.

The amendment created the post of Chief of Defence Forces, or CDF. The army chief holds it at the same time. A key duty is jointness, which means the army, navy and air force plan and fight as a team.

It also closed an old office. Since 1976, the Chairman Joint Chiefs of Staff Committee had coordinated the 3 services.

The last chairman, General Sahir Shamshad Mirza, retired on 27 November 2025. The post ended with him.

On 4 December 2025, the President approved Field Marshal Munir as the first CDF, for 5 years. The defence ministry made it official the next day. Air Chief Marshal Zaheer Ahmed Babar Sidhu was also given 2 more years in office, starting 19 March 2026.

The navy stayed steady. Admiral Naveed Ashraf has led it since 7 October 2023.

On 24 July 2026, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif promoted Lieutenant General Aamer Raza to full general. Raza was then the army's Chief of General Staff. He became the first Commander of the National Strategic Command.

This command oversees Pakistan's strategic forces. It took over the nuclear role that the old chairman's office used to handle. Its commander serves 3 years and is chosen on the CDF's advice.

Parliament then filled in the details. On 20 August 2026, it passed the Defence Forces of Pakistan Act. This law creates a Defence Forces Headquarters under the CDF, who becomes the prime minister's main military adviser.

The same law makes the CDF responsible to the federal government for command and control. A second law updated the National Command Authority Act, the law on nuclear command, to include the new posts.

Supporters call this modernisation, or bringing the system up to date, for wars that move at digital speed. Critics, Dawn reported, warn about centralisation, which means too much power in a single place.

Dawn also noted a cost. For nearly 5 decades, the navy and air force had a formal seat at the top. That seat is now gone.

In his farewell, General Mirza called jointness between the services a compulsion, not a choice.

On 2 October 2026, the army's top commanders met at GHQ for their 277th Corps Commanders' Conference. The Field Marshal told them to absorb modern technology, improve training and integrate all areas of war.

That is the real test. A new chart on the wall does not win battles. Trained people who trust each other across all 3 services do.

Points to remember.

General Asim Munir was promoted to Field Marshal on 20 May 2025.
The 27th Amendment of 13 November 2025 created the Chief of Defence Forces post and ended the Chairman Joint Chiefs post on 27 November 2025.
Field Marshal Munir was notified as the first CDF, and still army chief, for 5 years on 5 December 2025.
Admiral Naveed Ashraf has led the navy since 7 October 2023. Air Chief Marshal Zaheer Ahmed Babar Sidhu has 2 more years from 19 March 2026.
General Syed Aamer Raza became the first Commander National Strategic Command on 24 July 2026.
The Defence Forces of Pakistan Act of 20 August 2026 set up a Defence Forces Headquarters under the CDF.
