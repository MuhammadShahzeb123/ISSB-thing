Pakistan's best Asian Games since 2002, but no gold.

On 3 October 2026, in Japan, Hasan Nawaz kept hitting the ball over the boundary. He made 96 not out from 51 balls, with 7 sixes, in the Asian Games cricket final against India.

It was not quite enough. India made 211 and Pakistan reached 192, so Pakistan lost by 19 runs. Still, it was Pakistan's first ever silver medal in Asian Games men's cricket.

The Asian Games are Asia's biggest multi-sport event. The 2026 Games were held in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, from 19 September to 4 October 2026.

Pakistan finished with 10 medals, 2 silver and 8 bronze. It was the country's best total since 2002, when it won 13. At the previous Games in Hangzhou, it won only 3.

The medals came from many sports. The hockey team had already played its first World Cup in 8 years in August 2026, finishing 11th.

In Japan, on 1 October 2026, the team fought back from 3 goals down against India. It then lost the semi-final 4 goals to 3 in the last minute.

On 3 October 2026, the hockey players beat South Korea 5 goals to 3 for bronze. The same day, the volleyball team beat China 3 sets to 0. It was Pakistan's first Asian Games volleyball medal since 1962.

In squash, Noor Zaman and Mohammad Ashab Irfan won singles bronze, and the team won silver. More bronze came in women's cricket, shooting, mixed martial arts and PUBG Mobile, a video game played as a sport.

The hardest story belonged to Arshad Nadeem. In Paris in 2024, he won Olympic gold with a record throw of 92.97 metres.

In Nagoya on 28 September 2026, on a wet and slippery runway, he threw 80.29 metres and finished 4th.

His body has struggled since Paris. He won the Asian Championships in May 2025 with 86.40 metres. After calf surgery in July 2025, he came 10th at the World Championships in Tokyo.

Then he finished 9th at the Commonwealth Games in Glasgow on 31 July 2026.

Cricket fans have had a hard 18 months as well. In February 2025, Pakistan hosted the Champions Trophy, the first major cricket event at home in 29 years. The team went out in the group stage.

On 28 February 2026, Pakistan left the T20 World Cup on net run rate, a tie-breaker based on scoring speed. Opener Sahibzada Farhan still scored 383 runs, a record for 1 T20 World Cup.

Why does this matter? Sport is where many young Pakistanis first learn discipline and teamwork. A volleyball medal after 64 years shows that smaller sports can rise with steady training.

A fair view sees both sides. 10 medals are real progress, but Pakistan has not won an Asian Games gold since 2014.

Athletes need good coaches, fitness support and long-term planning. They need support in bad seasons, not only praise after wins and anger after losses.

Arshad's 4th place and the volleyball bronze teach the same lesson. Medals are won in training halls years before the final, and that is where Pakistan must now invest.
