Pakistan and Bangladesh, talking like brothers again.

Late on 29 January 2026, a Biman Bangladesh Airlines plane landed in Karachi. It received a water cannon salute, a traditional welcome for a special flight. Every seat on the flight from Dhaka was full.

It was the first direct passenger flight between the 2 countries in many years. The trip now takes about 3 hours, with no stop in Dubai or Doha.

Behind this moment is a painful history. Bangladesh was East Pakistan until 1971, when it became independent after a war. Under Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina, who ruled for 15 years, ties with Pakistan stayed cold.

Things changed on 5 August 2024. A student-led uprising removed Hasina, and she fled to India. An interim government under Nobel winner Muhammad Yunus took charge and reached out to Pakistan.

The changes came step by step. In November 2024, a cargo ship sailed directly from Karachi to Chittagong, the first in decades. In December 2024, Bangladesh removed an extra security check for Pakistani visa applicants.

In February 2025, the first ship carrying Pakistani rice left for Bangladesh under a deal for 50,000 tonnes. It was the first government-to-government trade between the 2 countries since 1971.

On 23 and 24 August 2025, Ishaq Dar became the first Pakistani foreign minister to visit Dhaka in about 13 years. The 2 sides signed 6 documents. Pakistan offered 500 scholarships to Bangladeshi students over 5 years.

The past did not disappear. Bangladesh has asked for an apology for 1971, a share of old assets, and the return of stranded Pakistanis. Dar said some of these issues were settled in 1974 and again in the early 2000s. Dhaka disagreed, but both sides agreed to keep talking.

Then came the election of 12 February 2026. The Bangladesh Nationalist Party, or BNP, and its allies won 212 seats. Its leader Tarique Rahman became prime minister on 17 February 2026.

Pakistan moved quickly. Shehbaz Sharif phoned Rahman on 13 February 2026 and invited him to visit Pakistan. India's Prime Minister Narendra Modi also called Rahman within hours of the result.

Dhaka now calls its foreign policy "Bangladesh First". It says it will balance its ties with all partners.

The friendship keeps growing. A Bangladeshi diplomat said in May 2026 that trade had risen 20 percent to about 1 billion dollars. On 24 September 2026, Shehbaz Sharif and Tarique Rahman met in New York and agreed to expand trade, education and tourism.

Trade still needs balance. Pakistan sells Bangladesh far more than it buys. Dhaka has asked Pakistan to remove import taxes on its jute.

The wise way forward is patience and respect. Bangladesh is a proud and sovereign nation, and it will make its own choices. Pakistan can earn trust through fair trade, scholarships and honest talk about the past.

Once, these 2 Muslim nations separated in pain. Today planes and ships link Karachi and Dhaka again. Now they must carry trust, not only passengers and rice.

Points to remember.

Sheikh Hasina fell on 5 August 2024. Muhammad Yunus then led an interim government.
A deal for 50,000 tonnes of Pakistani rice in February 2025 was the first government-to-government trade since 1971.
Ishaq Dar visited Dhaka on 23 and 24 August 2025 and offered 500 scholarships.
Direct Dhaka to Karachi flights began on 29 January 2026.
Tarique Rahman became prime minister on 17 February 2026 and met Shehbaz Sharif in New York on 24 September 2026.
