Champions of twenty twenty five and twenty twenty six.

Sport questions are the easiest marks in a general knowledge test, if you know who lifted the trophy. Here are the big winners.

In early twenty twenty five, Pakistan hosted the Champions Trophy, its first big cricket event in nearly thirty years. India played its matches in Dubai, and won the final there, beating New Zealand by four wickets.

In September, India beat Pakistan by five wickets in the Asia Cup final in Dubai. But in December, Pakistan's young players hit back. In the Under nineteen Asia Cup final, Pakistan beat India by one hundred and ninety one runs, and Sameer Minhas scored a stunning one hundred and seventy two.

In women's cricket, India won its first fifty over World Cup in November twenty twenty five, beating South Africa. In July twenty twenty six, Australia beat England at Lord's to win the women's T twenty World Cup for the seventh time.

India also won the men's T twenty World Cup in March twenty twenty six, beating New Zealand by ninety six runs in Ahmedabad.

At home, Lahore Qalandars won the tenth season of the Pakistan Super League in twenty twenty five. In twenty twenty six, Peshawar Zalmi beat the new team Hyderabad Kingsmen, and lifted their second title. In India, Royal Challengers Bengaluru won their first IPL.

In football, the twenty twenty six World Cup was the first with forty eight teams, hosted by the United States, Canada and Mexico. In the final in July, Spain beat Argentina one nil after extra time. Ferran Torres scored, and Spain won their second World Cup.

In tennis, twenty twenty five belonged to two young men. Jannik Sinner won the Australian Open and Wimbledon. Carlos Alcaraz won the French Open and the US Open. Among women, Madison Keys, Coco Gauff, Iga Swiatek and Aryna Sabalenka each won one.

Why does sport matter in an interview? It shows you follow the world beyond your books. And teamwork on the field is a lesson every officer understands.
