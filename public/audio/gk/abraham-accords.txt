The Abraham Accords, peace without Palestine.

In September 2020, on the White House lawn, the UAE and Bahrain signed papers with Israel. Morocco followed that December, and Sudan signed in January 2021, though its ties never really began. They were the first Arab states to recognise Israel since Jordan in 1994. The deals were named after the Prophet Ibrahim, honoured by Jews, Christians and Muslims alike.

For years the Arab world had held one promise. In 2002 it offered Israel full normal ties, but only after a Palestinian state was born on the 1967 lines with East Jerusalem as its capital. Land for peace. The Accords turned that around. Recognition came first, and Palestine was left for later.

The signatories did gain things. Flights, trade, investment and security ties, much of it aimed at Iran. Israel put its plan to annex the West Bank on hold. But Palestinians got nothing, and the pressure on Israel eased. In 2025 alone Israel approved a record 54 new settlements.

Then came October 2023. After the Hamas attack, Israel's war on Gaza followed. By October 2026, Gaza's Health Ministry counted more than 74,000 Palestinians killed. The World Court ordered Israel to prevent genocide and later called the occupation unlawful. The International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Netanyahu and his former defence minister.

Muslim leaders now speak in the harshest words. Türkiye's President Erdogan called Israel a terror state. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif told the United Nations that Israel follows a genocidal agenda. Israel denies these charges, but the court rulings make them hard to dismiss.

Seen against this record, the Accords look like a reward for occupation. Saudi Arabia froze its own talks. Ordinary people across the Arab world stayed angry with their rulers. And when war with Iran came in 2026, missiles fell on Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Closeness to Israel did not buy calm.

Pakistan never joined, and says it never will on these terms. Our Foreign Office and Ishaq Dar have said it clearly in 2026. No recognition until a free Palestinian state stands on the 1967 borders, with Al Quds Al Sharif as its capital.

The lesson is simple. Peace that skips the people at the heart of a conflict is not peace at all. It is only a pause.

Remember. 2020, the UAE and Bahrain, then Morocco and Sudan. Recognition before a Palestinian state. Pakistan says no until Palestine is free.
